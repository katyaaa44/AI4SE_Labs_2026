APE
source https://frompting.com/frameworks


I chose the APE framework because it is simple and clearly separates important parts of the prompt. This structure is suitable for the task because it lets me specify what the model should do, why it should do it, and what the final output should look like.
I expected APE to produce valid SQL queries for all questions and to follow the schema and output format correctly.
